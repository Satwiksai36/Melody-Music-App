## Brand & Style

This design system celebrates South Asian musical exuberance—centering on Telugu and Bollywood soundscapes—through an energetic blend of retro-modern editorial composition, cutout artist portraits, tactile playback controls, and warm organic shapes. 

The aesthetic balances playful contemporary warmth with high-end curation. Instead of standard dark utility streaming canvases, it utilizes rich royal electric purple backdrop accents paired with soft, inviting butter-cream surface plates. Tactile pill navigation, rounded thumbnail cards with pastel gradients, expressive typographic contrast, and floating glass playback elements evoke an emotional state of joy, rhythm, and discovery.

Key aesthetic pillars:
- **Editorial Collage & Cutouts:** Monochrome and duo-tone artist cutouts floating over curved mint, salmon, coral, and canary yellow geometric backdrop plates.
- **Warm Tactile Surfaces:** Soft eggshell and creamy butter containers that ground vibrant electric purple backgrounds, giving high tactile comfort during long listening sessions.
- **Rhythmic Micro-Interactions:** Squoval and pill badges, waveform audio meters, and prominent circular primary playback controls designed to feel physical and immediate.

## Colors

The palette combines an electric violet signature with warm editorial warmth and vibrant Bollywood/Tollywood energetic accents:

- **Primary (`#5B42F3`):** Electric Royal Violet used for active states, key brand headers, immersive playback gradients, and primary category tabs.
- **Secondary (`#FF5E3A`):** Vivid Coral / Tangerine for actionable playback moments (scrubber progress, play triggers, live tags, favorite indicators).
- **Tertiary (`#A8E6CF`):** Soft Mint / Pistachio for secondary chips, card backdrops, playlist genres, and chill listening badges.
- **Neutral (`#181824`):** Deep Ink Navy/Black providing crisp typographic hierarchy and high-contrast icon rendering.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - Base App Canvas: `#FDFBF7` (Warm Butter Linen)
  - Elevated Cards / Containers: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp White)
  - Sunken / Inset Track Wells: `#F4EFE6` (Subtle Biscuit)
  - Immersive Backdrop Mode: Deep Indigo Violet (`#1F1B2E`) used during full-screen player immersion.

## Typography

The typography couples the modern geometric boldness of **Outfit** for editorial titles and song names with the humanistic, legible warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body text, lyrics, credits, and metadata.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in Outfit with tight negative tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em) to yield a punchy, magazine-cover style seen in leading modern audio streaming interfaces.
- **Body & Metadata:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with generous line heights and open counters, ensuring instant legibility of bilingual song titles, playback duration numbers, and artist biographies.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered with semi-bold to bold weights, often configured in uppercase with slight positive tracking for genre tags (e.g., "TELUGU CHARTBUSTERS", "BOLLYWOOD RETRO").

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an adaptive fluid grid structured around 4 columns on mobile, 8 columns on tablet, and 12 columns on desktop. 

- **Vertical Cadence:** A strict 4px/8px rhythm ensures snug alignment between album artwork, track names, and audio controls.
- **Horizontal Carousels:** Category cards, daily mixes, and top charts extend gracefully past mobile margins with `gutter` spacing between items and trailing peek affordances.
- **Bottom Navigation Clearances:** All scrollable content retains an extra `4.5rem` bottom spacer to account for the persistent floating mini-player and bottom navigation dock without clipping content.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines warm surface layering with ambient, colorful shadows rather than harsh neutral drops:

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary viewport rests on a warm ivory canvas (`#FDFBF7`). Sheet overlays and full player sheets glide in on pure white cards (`#FFFFFF`) with smooth rounded corners.
- **Tinted Ambient Shadows:** 
  - Primary interactive cards utilize a soft, colored blur: `0px 10px 24px rgba(91, 66, 243, 0.08)`.
  - Floating playback buttons and mini-players utilize an energetic lift: `0px 8px 20px rgba(255, 94, 58, 0.22)`.
- **Frosted Translucency (Glassmorphism):**
  - The floating bottom mini-player uses `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a `16px` backdrop blur and a crisp `1px` translucent border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`).
- **Subtle Ghost Borders:** Outlines on cards and badges remain very light (`rgba(24, 24, 36, 0.06)`), retaining clarity without boxing in the expressive artwork.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes ultra-friendly, continuous curves and pill geometries:

- **Pill Philosophy:** Badges, filter chips, category tabs, and action buttons are fully pill-shaped (`9999px` border radius).
- **Squoval Containers:** Track covers, trending hero banners, and artist card portraits feature a generous `1.5rem` to `2rem` curvature, reflecting the soft, organic visual style of the reference mockups.
- **Asymmetric Graphic Arcs:** Decorative backdrop panels behind cutout artist images use arch silhouettes and circular halo accents to celebrate individual performers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Play Button:** Circular or pill-shaped, coated in `#FF5E3A` or `#5B42F3` with high-contrast white iconography. On tap, applies a slight spring scale (0.96x).
- **Follow / Action Pill:** Deep Ink (`#181824`) fill with crisp white typography, `1rem` vertical padding, and `1.5rem` horizontal padding.
- **Secondary Ghost Pill:** Pure white surface with a delicate 1px border (`rgba(24, 24, 36, 0.12)`) and `#181824` label text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Genre & Filter Chips:** Full-pill shape with pastel fills (`#A8E6CF` mint, `#FFE5B4` peach, `#E8E2FF` lavender). Active chips toggle to solid `#5B42F3` with white text.
- **Status & Duration Pills:** Inset biscuit background (`#F4EFE6`) with dark label text, placed unobtrusively beside track durations or episode counts.

### Track Lists & Row Items
- **Song Item:** Features a `48px` or `56px` rounded thumbnail (`rounded-xl`), title in `title-md`, artist and movie name in `body-sm` (`rgba(24, 24, 36, 0.6)`), and an explicit small circular play button on the trailing end.
- **Active / Now-Playing Row:** Fills with a gentle tint (`#F0EDFF`), displaying an animated coral waveform visualizer in place of the track index number.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Search Bar:** Pill-shaped text input with a soft cream-tinted background (`#F4EFE6`), subtle search icon, clear voice search button, and no harsh stroke.

### Cards & Discovery Heroes
- **Artist Spotlight Card:** Split-tone background composed of arched mint (`#A8E6CF`) or sunny yellow shapes with a cutout monochromatic/duotone artist photography asset bursting slightly outside the top frame.
- **Album / Playlist Card:** Soft square (`rounded-xl`), layered stacked effect underneath to hint at multiple tracks inside.

### Playback Controls & Waveform Scrubber
- **Tactile Waveform:** Interactive audio scrubber rendered as dynamic vertical equalizer bars that shift from light cream to `#FF5E3A` as the track progresses.
- **Floating Mini-Player:** Suspended glass container anchored `16px` above the bottom tab bar, showcasing a rotating mini disk or cover art, marquee track title, and quick play/skip triggers.
